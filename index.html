How to use
  1. Pick the container. Adjust payload to the CSC plate figure.
  2. Tap a cargo line to open it. Enter the size and weight of one unit and the quantity.
  3. Choose Stacking and Max load on top (total weight one unit can carry; blank = no limit).
  4. Choose Rotation: Fixed keeps the length along the container; Side up allows turning on the floor; Any allows tipping.
  5. The plan updates as you type. Check Results, then follow the Steps from the front wall to the doors.
  6. Save on this device, or download a PDF or plan file to share.
